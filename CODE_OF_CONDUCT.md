# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive and welcoming for contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and code with patience and technical clarity.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Acknowledge trade-offs and differing educational or engineering goals.
- Protect private, confidential and sensitive information.
- Accept corrections gracefully and help improve shared understanding.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or other conduct that makes participation unsafe or hostile is not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other project-related communication.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed in good faith, with appropriate confidentiality and proportionate action.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by widely used open-source community conduct standards and is intentionally written in concise project-specific language.
